About the AI Visibility Grader

A free tool by AKCLNT

What this tool does

The AI Visibility Grader reads your public website and scores how visible your business is to AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. You get an A–F grade across 12 checks, each with a plain-English explanation and one clear fix.

Why it matters

More and more customers skip Google and ask an AI assistant directly: "find me a dentist nearby" or "which plumber has the best reviews?" AI assistants can only recommend businesses they can understand (clear business info on your site) and trust (reviews, fresh content, secure site). If your site is missing those signals, you're invisible — no matter how good your business is.

How the grade is calculated — honestly

Let's be straight with you: this is not a live ChatGPT query. Nobody outside the AI companies can see exactly how their answers are chosen. Instead, we check the public signals that every published guide on AI search visibility agrees matter:

  • Structured business data (Schema.org markup)
  • FAQ and question-answer content
  • Clear titles and descriptions
  • Visible phone number and address
  • Customer reviews and testimonials
  • About and contact pages
  • Secure (HTTPS) website and fresh content

Each check is pass or fail. Your score is the share of checks passed, scaled to 0–100. Fix the signals, and real AI mentions follow — that's the whole game.

What we don't do

  • We don't store your URL or results on any server. Your last score is saved only in your own browser.
  • We don't invent statistics or guarantee rankings.
  • We don't sell your data — there is no account, no tracking, no signup.

Who made this

Built by AKCLNT, a software studio that builds websites, AI chatbots and automation for small businesses. We made this free because AI visibility is the next big shift in how customers find businesses — and most small businesses have no idea where they stand.
