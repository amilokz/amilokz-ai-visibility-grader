About

AI visibility, explained honestly.

What this tool does, why it matters for your business, and exactly how your grade is calculated — no black boxes.

What this tool does

It reads your public website and scores how visible your business is to AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. You get an A–F grade across 12 checks — each with a plain-English explanation and one clear fix.

Why it matters

Customers increasingly skip Google and ask an AI assistant directly: "find me a dentist nearby." AI can only recommend businesses it can understand (clear info on your site) and trust (reviews, fresh content, secure site). Missing those signals means you're invisible — no matter how good your business is.

How the grade is calculated — honestly

Let's be straight: this is not a live ChatGPT query. Nobody outside the AI companies can see exactly how answers are chosen. Instead, we check the public signals every published guide on AI search visibility agrees matter. Each check is pass or fail — your score is the share passed, scaled to 0–100.

✓Structured business data (Schema.org)
✓FAQ & question-answer content
✓Clear titles & descriptions
✓Visible phone & address
✓Reviews & testimonials
✓About & contact pages
✓HTTPS & fresh content

What we don't do

  • ✕No servers storing your URL or results — your last score lives only in your browser.
  • ✕No invented statistics, no guaranteed rankings.
  • ✕No selling your data — no account, no tracking, no signup.
