What Is AI Search Visibility? (Simple Explanation)

Guide · 4 min read

It used to be simple: a customer needed a plumber, they Googled "plumber near me," and clicked one of the results. Today, more and more people skip Google entirely and ask an AI assistant instead: "Hey ChatGPT, find me a reliable plumber nearby."

AI search visibility means: when someone asks an AI assistant for a business like yours, does it mention you?

How is this different from Google rankings?

On Google, ten businesses can share page one. With AI, the assistant usually names two or three — sometimes just one. There's no page two. You're either mentioned or you're invisible.

AI assistants also don't "rank" websites the way Google does. They read and summarize. They pick businesses they can clearly understand (what do you do, where are you, when are you open?) and trust (reviews, real content, a professional website).

What do AI assistants look at?

  • Structured business data — code on your site (called "schema markup") that states your name, address, hours and services in a format machines read easily.
  • Reviews — businesses with visible, recent reviews get recommended far more often.
  • Clear content — pages that plainly say what you do, written as questions and answers when possible.
  • Freshness — a site updated this year beats one untouched since 2021.
