Schema Markup for AI Visibility (Beginner's Guide)

Guide · 5 min read

What is schema markup?

Imagine your website is a person introducing themselves. Without schema, the introduction sounds like: "Hi, I'm... uh... we do stuff." With schema, it sounds like: "Hi, I'm Sunny Dental Clinic, a dentist at 123 Main Street, Austin, open Monday to Friday 9–6, phone 555-1234."

Schema markup is a small block of code that states the facts about your business in a format Google and AI assistants read directly. Humans never see it — machines do.

Why AI assistants depend on it

When someone asks ChatGPT "find a dentist near me," the AI needs facts: names, addresses, hours, services. Websites with schema hand over those facts on a plate. Websites without it force the AI to guess from paragraphs of marketing text — and AI doesn't guess, it moves on to a competitor that made it easy.

What should your schema include?

  • Business name and type (dentist, restaurant, plumber…)
  • Full street address
  • Phone number
  • Opening hours
  • Website URL and a short description

How to add it (10 minutes, no coding)

  1. Open our free Schema Generator.
  2. Fill in your business details.
  3. Click generate, then copy the code.
  4. Paste it into the <head> section of your homepage. (In WordPress: use a plugin like "Insert Headers and Footers." Or ask your developer — it's a 2-minute job.)
  5. Re-run the AI Visibility Grader — the schema check should turn green.

Common mistakes

  • Wrong business type — pick the closest match (Dentist, not generic LocalBusiness, if you're a dentist).
  • Outdated hours — update schema when hours change, especially holidays.
  • Only on the homepage — ideally every page carries it, but homepage-first is fine to start.
